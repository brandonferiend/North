Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

North is designed to help you manage your personal finances while respecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use North.

Information You Enter Into North

North allows you to enter and manage financial information such as accounts, balances, transactions, budgets, savings goals, categories, recurring transactions, loans, and related notes or details.

This information is used to provide North's financial tracking, budgeting, reporting, syncing, and sharing features.

iCloud and CloudKit

North uses Apple's iCloud and CloudKit services to support features such as syncing data between your Apple devices and sharing financial spaces with people you choose.

Data synced through iCloud is associated with the iCloud services provided by Apple and is subject to Apple's applicable privacy and security practices.

Shared Financial Spaces

If you choose to share a financial space with another person, information contained in that shared space may be available to participants you invite. You control whether you choose to participate in these sharing features.

North+ and Purchases

North offers optional North+ subscriptions through Apple's App Store. Purchases, subscription billing, and payment processing are handled by Apple.

North does not receive or store your full payment card or bank account information as part of processing an App Store purchase.

Advertising and Tracking

North does not display third-party advertisements and does not use your financial information for third-party advertising.

North does not use third-party advertising trackers to track your activity across apps or websites.

Analytics

North does not use third-party analytics services to build advertising profiles or sell your financial information.

Data Sharing

North does not sell your personal or financial information.

Information may be processed through Apple services when necessary to provide features you choose to use, including iCloud/CloudKit syncing, sharing, and App Store purchases.

Data Retention and Deletion

Financial information in North remains available as necessary to provide the app's features until it is removed through North or the applicable Apple/iCloud services.

You can delete financial information within North using the deletion controls available in the app. Data participating in iCloud syncing may also be synchronized across devices associated with the relevant iCloud account or shared space.

Your Choices

North's sharing features are optional. You choose whether to create or participate in shared financial spaces and which information you enter into North.

You can manage your North+ subscription through your Apple account's subscription settings.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as North changes or new features are introduced. The latest version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
