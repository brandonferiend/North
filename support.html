North+

North+ subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID. You can manage or cancel your subscription from your Apple subscription settings.

If you've previously purchased North+ and your subscription isn't being recognized, use Restore Purchases inside North.

iCloud & Sync

North uses iCloud to support syncing and shared financial spaces. Make sure you're signed into iCloud on your device and that iCloud access is available for North.
